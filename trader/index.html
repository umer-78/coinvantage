AI trading simulator

An AI trader that buys and sells with simulated money on live prices, using rules tested walk-forward on every coin it can trade, fees included. It places no orders on any exchange.

On each chart the page shows what the same rules made in the test, the worst drop, and what simply holding the coins made. In the latest walk-forward test (2026-10-24, 106 coins, fees included) the rules lost money on 2 of the 4 charts tested, and holding the same coins did better on every chart. The page shows both, each with its worst drop.

  • What it does

    Runs a demo account you trade by hand, an AI account that follows rules automatically, and a record of real trades you placed elsewhere, all measured the same way.

  • What it measures

    Return after fees, worst drop (open trades included), number of trades and win rate, next to what holding the same coins did over the same periods.

  • What it does not do

    It never places a real order, holds no funds or exchange keys, and is not a path to profit. Simulated results are not real results.

  • Limitations

    Simulated orders fill at the candle price. Real orders pay spread and slippage, can fill partly or not at all, and exchanges can halt. Custom rules were not part of the test.

Accounts and strategies, explained

  • Demo account: simulated money you trade by hand, to practise without risk.
  • AI account: simulated money traded by the rules you choose, on live prices, in your browser and in the cloud when you are signed in.
  • Real account: a record of trades you placed yourself on an exchange, entered by hand or imported. The app does not place them.
  • Tested default strategy: the rules for each chart that went through the walk-forward test with fees and fill costs counted. Their result is shown whether it is good or bad.
  • Custom strategy: your own settings. They were not tested, so the tested figures say nothing about them; a look back on the same history flatters them.
