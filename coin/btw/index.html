BTW price, chart and AI forecast

The live BTW price and candlestick chart come from Gate.io. When you open the page the app trains its forecast models on BTW's own candles, in your browser, and shows the price range they give for the next candles, the indicators and the AI trader's signal for BTW.

Its price range is measured: the 80% range held 79–80.1% of the time across about 540,000 tests on 106 coins.

No model has beaten chance at calling which way price goes next, so the page never presents a direction as a buy or sell call.

  • What it does

    Shows the live BTW price and chart, 13 indicators, a price-range forecast trained on BTW's own candles in your browser, and the AI trader's signal.

  • What it measures

    How often the forecast range held across the tested coins, and the AI trader's walk-forward result on each chart.

  • What it does not do

    It does not say whether to buy or sell BTW, place orders, or know about news before it happens.

  • Limitations

    Direction calls have not beaten a simple baseline, and every figure describes the past. Not financial advice.
